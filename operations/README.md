# Operations

This area contains **day-2 DBRE operating content**.

## Major subareas

- `sre/` — incident response and SRE-oriented runbooks
- `failover/` — HA/DR and failover guidance
- `backup/` — backup and restore procedures
- `replication/` — replication operations
- `maintenance/` — recurring maintenance playbooks
- `standards/` — operating standards and controls
- `templates/` — runbook and documentation templates
- `capacity/`, `indexing/`, `performance/`, `workload/` — operational review material
- `estate/` — estate-wide operating patterns

## Guidance

Put reusable runbooks, standards, and operational playbooks here rather than scattering them under engine-specific trees.
